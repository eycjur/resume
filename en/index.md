# Kmuto - Software Engineer @ RIKEN

After working on system development leveraging **machine learning and generative AI** and **AI governance** product development, I now work as a Software Engineer in **AI for Science**. I have consistently taken ownership of the full AI application lifecycle—from designing and implementing AI systems and algorithms to backend development, cloud infrastructure setup, and system deployment. I also actively share technical knowledge through OSS activities, participation in technical communities, and publishing technical books. I was born on 1999.

## Skills & Expertise

- **Data Science / Machine Learning**: Data preprocessing and analysis with Numpy/Pandas; building ML models with scikit-learn/LightGBM/PyTorch; hands-on MLOps experience with Vertex AI Pipeline and SageMaker Pipeline.
- **Generative AI / AI Agents**: Experience developing and fine-tuning LLMs, RAG, and AI agents using tools like LiteLLM and LangChain. Also participated in the Matsuo Lab LLM Development Competition 2025, contributing to foundation model training.
- **Backend**: API design and development with Python (FastAPI, Flask); experience with Golang.
- **Database**: Experience with PostgreSQL, FireStore, and CosmosDB; performance tuning knowledge.
- **Frontend**: SPA development with Vue.js/Svelte; rapid prototyping with Dash/Streamlit. Performance tuning using Lighthouse.
- **Cloud / Infrastructure**: Building applications on GCP and Azure; IaC with Terraform.
- **Security**: Knowledge of AI security; also participate in CTF competitions.
- **Design / Quality**: Design focused on separation of concerns, loose coupling, and modularity for high reusability and extensibility. Experience in designing and quality management for enterprise AI products with availability, maintainability, and fault tolerance in mind.
